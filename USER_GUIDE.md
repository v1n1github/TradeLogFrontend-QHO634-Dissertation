# TradeLog — Participant User Guide

Welcome to **TradeLog**, a trade journaling system designed to help you track your trades and reflect on your risk management behaviour.

This guide will walk you through everything you need to do as a study participant.

---

## Getting Started

The system should already be running on the researcher's machine. Open your browser and go to:

**http://localhost:3000**

You will see the **Dashboard** — this is the home screen.

---

## Step 1: Log a Trade

1. Click **"Add Trade"** in the navigation bar (or the button on the Dashboard).
2. Fill in the trade details:

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|--------------|
| **Trade Date** | The date you took the trade |
| **Instrument** | e.g. EUR/USD, XAUUSD, AAPL, BTC/USD |
| **Direction** | Long (buy) or Short (sell) |
| **Entry Price** | The price you entered at |
| **Stop Loss** | Your stop-loss level |
| **Take Profit** | Your target price (optional) |
| **Risk %** | The % of your account you risked (e.g. 1.0) |
| **R:R Ratio** | Auto-calculated if you filled SL and TP |
| **Outcome** | Win, Loss, or Breakeven |
| **Actual P&L** | The actual profit/loss amount (optional) |
| **Rule Adherence** | Tick this if the trade followed your rules |
| **Notes** | Any context, observations, or thoughts |

3. Click **"Save Trade & Add Reflection"**.

---

## Step 2: Add a Reflection

After saving a trade, you will be taken to the **Reflection page** for that trade.

This is where you record:

- **Emotional state** — How did you feel? (Calm, Anxious, Overconfident, etc.)
- **What went well** — Execution, patience, discipline
- **What could be improved** — Entry timing, position sizing, exit
- **Lesson learned** — Key takeaway for future trades

Reflection is optional but strongly encouraged — it is one of the most valuable parts of maintaining a trading journal.

Click **"Save Reflection"** when done, or **"Skip"** to go back to the trade log.

---

## Step 3: Explore the Dashboard

Click **"Dashboard"** in the navigation bar to see your performance metrics:

| Metric | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| **Total Trades** | Count of all trades logged |
| **Win Rate** | % of winning trades |
| **Net P&L** | Total profit/loss |
| **Profit Factor** | Gross wins ÷ gross losses |
| **Avg R:R** | Average risk-to-reward ratio |
| **Avg Risk %** | Your average risk per trade |
| **Risk Consistency** | How consistent your risk-sizing is (lower = more consistent) |
| **Rule Adherence** | % of trades where you followed your rules |

The **charts** below the stats show:
- Win/Loss/Breakeven distribution (pie chart)
- Risk % trend over time (line chart)
- Risk % per trade coloured by outcome (bar chart)

---

## Step 4: Review and Edit Trades

Click **"Trade Log"** to see all your trades in a table.

- **Sort** columns by clicking the column header
- **Search** by instrument name using the search box
- **Edit** a trade by hovering and clicking "Edit"
- **Delete** a trade by hovering and clicking "Del"
- **Add/view reflections** by clicking the reflection link in the table

---

## Step 5: Complete the Evaluation

Once you have finished using the system (after logging several trades and exploring the dashboard), please complete the **Evaluation** form.

Click **"Evaluation"** in the navigation bar.

The evaluation takes approximately **3–5 minutes** and includes:

- 4 rating questions (1–5 scale)
- 3 open-ended questions

If given a **Participant ID** by the researcher, please enter it at the top of the form.

---

## Tips

- **Be honest** with the Rule Adherence checkbox — it helps you identify patterns in your trading discipline.
- **Log every trade** during the study period, including losses and mistakes.
- The **Notes** field is useful for recording your reasoning at the time of the trade.
- Reflections are most useful when written soon after the trade closes.
